# Black Tower v1.2

## Campaign expansion

The campaign now enters the Black Tower after the Ash Colossus falls.

The Lower Spire is designed as a more vertical-feeling dungeon inside the existing side-view action framework.

## Tower traversal

New traversal systems:
- Arcane elevators that move between lower and upper routes.
- Phase platforms that visibly destabilize before becoming non-solid.
- Layered tower architecture, conduits, windows, moving magical motes, and a deep central shaft.
- The Black Tower soundtrack replaces the mountain cue once the player crosses the tower threshold.

## Mirror Vault puzzle

Three Arcane Mirrors seal the route to the Upper Spire.

The story clue is:

- **Moon mirror** → UP
- **Heart mirror** → RIGHT
- **Road mirror** → LEFT

Each mirror rotates clockwise when the player interacts with it.

The door opens only when all three mirrors agree with their target directions.

The solved state persists through Continue.

## Seventh champion — Fighter

Fighter is a technical melee champion built around timing and counterplay.

### Resource
**Momentum**

Momentum is earned by:
- landing weapon strikes,
- taking damage,
- successful counters,
- collecting Arcane Shards.

### Kit
- technical three-hit sword chain,
- heavy Breaker Thrust,
- Combat Roll,
- Parry,
- Riposte,
- Relentless Assault.

### Parry
A short timing window completely negates an incoming hit, grants Momentum, and automatically triggers a counterstrike.

### Riposte
Consumes Momentum for an invulnerable forward thrust with high damage, knockback, and stun.

### Relentless Assault
Consumes full Momentum and performs a rapid sequence of alternating strikes followed by a heavy finisher.

## Seventh shrine blessing

**Steadfast** reduces incoming knockback by 25%.

## Tower enemies

### Void Sentry
Fast melee pressure with short-range phase movement.

### Arcane Eye
Ranged floating caster that tries to maintain space.

### Shadow Knight
Heavy armored melee enemy with stronger resistance to knockback and stun.

## Boss — Obsidian Warden

The Warden guards the Crown Seal.

It uses:
- close-range cleaves,
- homing orb spreads,
- void-wave attacks,
- blink repositioning,
- temporary phase barriers,
- three phases at 62% and 28% health.

The Warden's role is intentionally different from the true Evil Wizard. Defeating it breaks the final seal and opens the route to the Crown Chamber.

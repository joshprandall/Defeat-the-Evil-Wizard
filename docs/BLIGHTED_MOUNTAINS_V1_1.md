# Blighted Mountains v1.1

## Campaign expansion

The Last Road now continues through the Memory Gate and into the Blighted Mountains.

This region is built around **motion under pressure** rather than another flat combat corridor.

### Environmental systems

- **High-wind zones** push the champion horizontally and alter aerial control.
- **Collapsing ledges** warn, crack, fall, and rebuild after a delay.
- The ascent repeatedly combines enemies with unstable footing.
- The Black Tower becomes physically visible at the far side of the region.

## Storm Vane puzzle

Three ancient vanes seal the summit road.

The story clue is:

1. first vane → **rising sun** → RIGHT,
2. second vane → **setting sun** → LEFT,
3. third vane → **summit** → UP.

Each vane rotates clockwise when the player interacts with it.

When all three point correctly, the Storm Gate opens permanently and the solution is saved.

This puzzle is different from the Memory Labyrinth: it is spatial and symbolic rather than a simple ordered sequence.

## Sixth champion — Barbarian

Barbarian is the roster's heavy-aggression specialist.

### Resource
**Fury**

Fury is earned primarily by:
- dealing melee damage,
- taking damage,
- collecting Arcane Shards.

### Kit
- three-hit axe chain,
- Heavy Cleave,
- Reckless Leap-style dash,
- Ground Slam,
- Berserk,
- Worldbreaker.

### Berserk
Consumes Fury and temporarily increases damage to 140% of the champion's permanent damage modifier.

### Worldbreaker
Consumes full Fury and creates a large radial impact with high damage, knockback, and stun.

## Sixth shrine blessing

**Renewal** adds 25% to all healing received.

It extends shrine progression so the sixth campaign checkpoint always has a meaningful unclaimed blessing available.

## Mountain enemies

### Ash Hound
Fast rush attacker.

### Storm Shaman
Maintains range and fires magic projectiles.

### Stone Raider
Heavy melee defender with greater knockback/stun resistance.

## Regional boss — Ash Colossus

The Ash Colossus uses:
- stone slams,
- armored rushes,
- thrown rolling boulders,
- second-phase damage mitigation,
- faster attack cadence below 50% health.

Defeating the Colossus reveals the Black Tower as the next major campaign destination.

## Sound additions

Seven new sound effects support the region:
- wind gust,
- rock crack,

- ground slam,
- stone roar,
- axe swing,
- rock impact.

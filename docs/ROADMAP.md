# Production Roadmap

## 0.4 — Fallen Village
First authored region, Arcane Shards, Grave Knight miniboss, campaign checkpoints, expanded environmental presentation.

## 0.5 — Persistence and progression
Save/continue, shrine blessing choices, relic inventory foundation, NPC dialogue, ranged enemy archetype, movement/combat lab.

## 0.6 — Champion architecture expansion
Status-effect framework, animation-state framework, additional champion archetypes, shared ability data, balance instrumentation.

## 0.7 — Whispering Woods
Second authored region, vertical traversal, environmental hazards, new enemy family, region miniboss, secrets.

## 0.8 — Sunken Keep and Blighted Mountains
Additional regions, expanded traversal mechanics, encounter variety, progression tuning, broader champion roster.

## 0.9 — Black Tower campaign completion
Final campaign structure, complete Wizard encounter presentation, remaining content, endings, full save flow.

## 1.0 — Release candidate
Final art, character animation, environment art, VFX, audio mix, accessibility, performance, controller polish, balance, bug fixing, packaging and release builds.

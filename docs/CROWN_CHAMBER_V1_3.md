# Crown Chamber v1.3

## Campaign finale milestone

The campaign now continues beyond the Obsidian Warden and enters the Crown Chamber.

This milestone completes the first playable campaign arc, but it is **not** the final production release. Art, animation, balancing, accessibility, all-class completion, and broad runtime testing remain future work.

## Eighth champion — Monk

Monk is a speed/combo champion.

### Resource
**Chi**

Chi is earned primarily by landing attacks and taking limited damage.

### Kit
- four-hit martial combo,
- Palm Strike,
- Wind Step,
- Flurry,
- Inner Peace,
- Hundred Hands.

### Inner Peace
Consumes Chi, heals the Monk, and temporarily reduces incoming damage and knockback.

### Hundred Hands
Consumes full Chi and performs a rapid sequence of twelve strikes followed by a heavy finisher.

## Eighth shrine blessing

**Ascension** guarantees that the champion respawns with at least 35% of their class resource.

This gives the final checkpoint a meaningful unique blessing without duplicating the earlier damage, defense, movement, healing, or resource-generation upgrades.

## Crown Chamber

The Crown Chamber introduces:
- cathedral-scale interior architecture,
- stained-glass story panels,
- a suspended magical crown,
- a final shrine,
- multi-level final-arena platforms,
- a dedicated Crown Chamber music cue.

## True Evil Wizard

The final Wizard is a separate encounter from the earlier Black Gate shadow.

### Phase I — The Duel
The Wizard uses aimed bolts, fan patterns, Arcane Lances, and Void Step.

### Phase II — The Crown Seal
At 70% health, three Crown Sigils appear.

While any sigil remains, the Wizard receives only 12% of normal incoming damage.

The player must destroy all three before the deeper phases can begin.

### Phase III — The Roads Return
At 38% health, the Wizard:
- casts denser projectile patterns,
- summons tower enemies,
- uses Crown Rain and void waves,
- teleports more frequently.

### Phase IV — Mortal Spell
At 14% health, the Wizard begins burning his own life to sustain the spell.

He loses 0.9% of maximum health per second while attacking at his fastest cadence.

Void Step itself also costs 3% of maximum health each time it is used.

This preserves the established rule that disappearing into the void is powerful but never free.

## Ending

Defeating the True Wizard triggers a full epilogue sequence.

If all five Arcane Shards were collected, an additional ending beat acknowledges their restoration across the realm.

The existing victory screen then closes the playable campaign.

# Art Direction

## North star

High-resolution 2D dark fantasy with painterly environments and crisp gameplay silhouettes. The target is atmospheric rather than muddy, dramatic rather than visually noisy.

## Palette logic

- World neutrals: charcoal, desaturated blue, stone green, iron gray.
- Human warmth: amber lanterns, parchment, muted skin tones, firelight.
- Hero interaction: warm gold/orange accents.
- Wizard corruption: violet/magenta with cold cyan highlights.
- Danger: readable red that is never the only cue.

## Silhouette rule

At gameplay zoom, the player, every enemy archetype, projectiles, hazards, pickups, and interactive objects must be identifiable without relying on interior detail.

## Environment layers

1. Sky and large celestial elements.
2. Distant terrain silhouette.
3. Midground architecture/forest masses.
4. Playable collision layer.
5. Foreground framing and weather.
6. Effects layer for combat and corruption.

Parallax depth must never interfere with platform readability.

## Animation standards

Player actions need anticipation, impact, recovery, and cancellation rules. Hits use short hit-stop, a restrained camera response, directional knockback, impact particles, sound, and a brief target flash.

## UI

Diegetic-dark framing with modern clarity. Health, class resource, cooldown state, boss phase, interaction prompts, and checkpoint status must be readable at a glance. Menus should look like the same world rather than an unrelated web interface.

## Asset policy

Prototype geometry is disposable. Production art should be created specifically for this game or come from assets whose licenses permit redistribution and commercial release. Track every external asset and license in an asset manifest.

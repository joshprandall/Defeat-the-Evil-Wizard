# Story & Labyrinth v1.0

## Design direction

The campaign now alternates between three rhythms:

1. **Combat regions** — movement, enemies, bosses.
2. **Narrative interludes** — short cinematic sequences that reveal the world and establish the next destination.
3. **Adventure spaces** — exploration, maze routing, environmental hazards, and puzzles.

This prevents the campaign from becoming one long horizontal combat corridor.

## Story structure

### Prologue
The champion enters a realm disappearing road by road. The Wizard invites the pursuit.

### Black Gate
The first Wizard battle is explicitly revealed as a projected shadow.

### Whispering Woods
Mara explains that the road continues beneath the roots into drowned halls.

### Sunken Keep
The Castellan's death exposes the concept of the Memory Labyrinth and reinforces the three historical symbols.

### Memory Labyrinth
The player must remember the realm's history rather than merely defeat another enemy.

## Puzzle language

The three runes are:
- **Bell** — Fallen Village / Mara.
- **Moon** — Whispering Woods.
- **Crown** — Sunken Keep / Castellan.

The solution is Bell → Moon → Crown.

The clue is intentionally redundant:
- story,
- mural,
- regional symbolism.

The puzzle therefore asks the player to observe and remember without becoming an obscure guessing exercise.

## Character animation direction

The current procedural characters now expose better readable poses and secondary details, but this is still an intermediate rendering layer.

The final authored-character pipeline should preserve:
- state names,
- hit windows,
- movement speed,
- jump timing,
- cancel timing,
- weapon reach.

Art follows the game feel, not the reverse.

## Next production target

The next region should be the **Blighted Mountains**, with elevation, wind, collapsing terrain, and larger-scale boss spectacle.

The Black Tower remains the campaign destination rather than the next immediate room.

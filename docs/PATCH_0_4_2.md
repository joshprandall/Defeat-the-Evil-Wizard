# Patch 0.4.2 — Shadow Step Costs Blood

The Evil Wizard now pays a much steeper price for disappearing into the void.

- A complete Shadow Step costs exactly 10% of the Wizard's maximum health.
- With 620 maximum health, a full vanish costs about 62 HP.
- The loss still drains continuously while he is absent rather than being deducted all at once.
- If the Wizard reappears early for any future reason, he only pays for the time actually spent vanished.
- The boss health bar continues to drain during the disappearance and the total `VOID COST` is shown when he returns.

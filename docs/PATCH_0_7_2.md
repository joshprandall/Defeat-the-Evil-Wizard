# Patch 0.7.2 — Mara, the Bellkeeper

The strange purple figure beside the player was Mara, the Bellkeeper — the campaign's first story NPC.

Her old placeholder presentation was ambiguous: it used the same primitive human proportions as playable heroes and placed a pulsing ring around her head. That could read as another character, a shield, a targeting indicator, or an unexplained effect.

0.7.2 gives Mara a distinct visual language:
- hooded cloak,
- bell staff,
- hanging bell,
- warm lantern,
- MARA / BELLKEEPER nameplate,
- explicit E-style interaction symbol,
- subtle idle sway,
- grounded shadow.

The old head halo is removed.

# Sunken Keep 0.8

## Region goal

The Sunken Keep changes traversal rhythm.

Fallen Village teaches the core.
Whispering Woods emphasizes mobility.
Sunken Keep adds environmental timing.

The player must now read enemies and the room itself.

## Environmental mechanics

### Retracting spikes
Periodic floor threat. The player can wait, jump, dash, or route around them.

### Arcane runes
Pulse on a readable rhythm and punish standing still.

### Ceiling crushers
Slow, dramatic timing hazards that reward committing through the opening rather than hesitating beneath them.

### Moving platforms
The first dynamic traversal surfaces in the campaign.

## Archer

Archer extends the shared Hero architecture into physical ranged combat.

Focus is earned by hitting enemies with projectiles. This intentionally rewards accuracy rather than passive waiting.

Piercing Arrow supports line-up play.
Arrow Rain creates a forward control zone.
Perfect Volley rewards positioning and a full Focus bar.

## Drowned Castellan

The Castellan is a heavyweight region boss.

Phase I:
- anchor strike,
- armored rush,
- tide volley.

Phase II:
- faster action selection,
- larger volleys,
- flood burst,
- modest damage mitigation.

The fight is designed to make every champion solve the same arena differently.
